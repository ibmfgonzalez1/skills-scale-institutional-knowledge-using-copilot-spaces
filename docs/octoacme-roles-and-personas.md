# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Program Managers / Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Program Managers or Delivery Leads coordinate multiple related projects, align cross-project dependencies, and support delivery at scale. They help connect strategy, execution, and stakeholder communication across several workstreams.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate capacity, dependencies, and sequencing across related projects
- Track milestones and progress against broader program goals
- Escalate cross-team constraints and organizational dependencies
- Align project delivery plans with leadership priorities and portfolio commitments
- Support governance, meeting cadence, and cross-functional decision-making

### Goals
- Improve delivery consistency across related initiatives
- Reduce coordination gaps between teams and stakeholders
- Maintain alignment between strategy, scope, and delivery reality

### Typical Communication
- Portfolio and leadership updates
- Cross-project dependency reviews
- Steering or governance meetings with sponsors and functional leads

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Project Managers to align schedules, risks, and communication rhythms
- Partners with Product Managers to ensure roadmap and delivery priorities remain coordinated
- Supports Developers and Engineering Leads by surfacing dependencies and reducing conflicting priorities

---

## Engineering Leads / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads provide technical direction, guide implementation trade-offs, and help teams deliver reliable solutions aligned to product goals. They serve as a bridge between architecture, delivery, and engineering quality.

### Responsibilities
- Own technical design guidance and implementation standards
- Help break down work into feasible delivery increments
- Identify architectural risks, dependencies, and technical debt
- Support estimation, sprint planning, and engineering readiness reviews
- Partner with Product and Project Managers on scope, sequencing, and trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, secure, and high-quality technical solutions
- Reduce avoidable rework and engineering bottlenecks
- Keep delivery aligned with technical feasibility and system health

### Typical Communication
- Architecture and design reviews
- Technical planning sessions and engineering standups
- Escalations for blockers, risk, and dependency management

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to clarify requirements, technical constraints, and implementation decisions
- Collaborates with Product Managers to evaluate trade-offs between value, risk, and delivery speed
- Provides updates to Project Managers on engineering delivery health and critical dependencies

---

## UX / Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX or Design Leads represent the user experience and ensure solutions are usable, accessible, and aligned with customer needs. They translate user feedback and product goals into clear design direction.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and interpret user needs, feedback, and usability insights
- Create or review UX flows, wireframes, and design patterns
- Ensure accessibility, clarity, and usability expectations are reflected in delivered work
- Partner with Product and Engineering on feature validation and iteration

### Goals
- Deliver solutions that are intuitive, accessible, and valuable to users
- Improve decision quality through user-centered design
- Ensure product requirements reflect real user behavior and needs

### Typical Communication
- Discovery and user research sessions
- Design reviews with product and engineering teams
- Feedback loops during demos and validation milestones

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate product problems and opportunity areas
- Collaborates with Developers to ensure implementation matches intended UX and design fidelity
- Supports QA in validating user journeys and acceptance criteria against real usability expectations

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA or Test Leads define quality expectations, validate delivery against acceptance criteria, and help reduce release risk. They ensure that work is testable, verified, and ready for stakeholder use.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage, and quality gates
- Coordinate validation across unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Track defects, defects trends, and regression risk
- Confirm release readiness against business and technical criteria
- Support teams in designing testable acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Minimize defects and release risk
- Improve confidence in product quality and stability
- Create repeatable quality practices across projects

### Typical Communication
- Test strategy and readiness reviews
- Defect triage and regression discussions
- Release signoff and quality check-ins

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to confirm quality expectations and testing coverage
- Partners with Product Managers to validate acceptance criteria and customer impact
- Coordinates with Project Managers and Release Leads on quality gates and readiness decisions

---

## Security / Privacy Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Partners help teams identify and mitigate vulnerabilities, compliance concerns, and data risks early in the lifecycle. They ensure that projects are built and operated in a way that protects customers and the business.

### Responsibilities
- Review security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Identify risks in architecture, data handling, and operational practices
- Recommend mitigations and control measures
- Participate in planning, design reviews, and pre-release validation
- Support incident response and post-incident learning when necessary

### Goals
- Reduce security and privacy exposure
- Build resilient, compliant solutions from the start
- Support safe and trusted product delivery

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and threat assessments
- Privacy and compliance check-ins
- Incident and risk escalation conversations

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Product Managers and Engineering Leads on risk trade-offs and mitigation options
- Works with Developers to ensure secure coding practices and control implementation
- Partners with Project Managers and Release Leads to verify risk is addressed before launch

---

## Release / DevOps or Site Reliability Leads

### Role Summary
Release, DevOps, or Site Reliability Leads make sure systems are deployable, observable, and recoverable. They help maintain stable delivery and operational readiness throughout the release lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Manage deployment pipelines, release windows, and rollback readiness
- Monitor service health, observability, and operational signals
- Prepare runbooks and recovery plans for incidents or degraded service
- Coordinate post-deployment validation and release communications
- Support reliability and performance improvements across critical workflows

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and operational disruption
- Increase confidence in deployment safety and recovery
- Maintain service quality after code reaches production

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment check-ins
- Incident response coordination and operational updates
- Post-release validation and follow-up communications

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers and QA to verify deployability and smoke-test readiness
- Partners with Project Managers to schedule releases and coordinate communication
- Collaborates with Product Managers and Stakeholders during release announcements and issue response

---

## Customer / Support Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer or Support Representatives bring real-world user feedback, support issues, and service impact into planning and prioritization. They help ensure that delivery decisions reflect what customers experience in practice.

### Responsibilities
- Surface common customer pain points and support trends
- Share feedback from support interactions, customer escalations, and user reports
- Help define service expectations and success criteria from the customer perspective
- Support enablement, communication, and follow-up after release

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and product usability
- Reduce avoidable support friction and escalations
- Align delivery work with real user impact

### Typical Communication
- Feedback reviews and customer issue summaries
- Support triage updates and service-impact discussions
- Release feedback loops and post-launch follow-up

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides evidence and context to Product Managers for prioritization decisions
- Helps Project Managers understand customer impact and communication needs
- Brings practical insight to Developers and QA when validating user problems and resolution quality

---

## Executive Sponsors / Business Owners

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors or Business Owners provide strategic direction, approve major priorities, and resolve sponsor-level issues that affect value delivery. They ensure the project remains aligned with business outcomes and investments.

### Responsibilities
- Set strategic priorities and sponsorship for important initiatives
- Approve scope, funding, and trade-offs at key decision points
- Help resolve escalations involving business impact, dependencies, or organizational change
- Confirm that delivery outcomes remain aligned with business objectives

### Goals
- Ensure investments deliver measurable business value
- Support organizational alignment and decision speed
- Maintain accountability for strategic outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee meetings
- Business reviews and milestone updates
- Sponsor-level escalation and decision communications

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides direction to Product Managers and Project Managers on priorities and trade-offs
- Reviews risk and readiness issues raised by delivery teams
- Helps align cross-functional work with broader business goals and stakeholder expectations

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles are intended to make project ownership, decision-making, and handoffs clearer across the complete lifecycle from initiation to release and continuous improvement.
