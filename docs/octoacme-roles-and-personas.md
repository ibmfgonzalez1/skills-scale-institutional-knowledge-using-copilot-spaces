# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers support sustainable delivery by aligning team capacity, technical execution, and execution quality with the project plan. They help connect engineering work to business priorities while protecting the health and focus of the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Balance workload and staffing across the engineering team
- Support sprint planning, resourcing, and delivery commitments
- Partner with Project Managers and Product Managers on feasibility and sequencing
- Coach team health, execution quality, and technical decision-making
- Help remove blockers that slow delivery or reduce team effectiveness

### Goals
- Maintain predictable delivery and engineering capacity
- Improve team health and delivery quality
- Reduce friction between product priorities and technical execution

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering and project planning meetings
- Capacity and staffing discussions with PMs and leadership
- Cross-team dependency updates and escalation reports

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate roadmap priorities into realistic delivery plans
- Collaborates with Project Managers to align commitments, milestones, and risk management
- Supports Developers by enabling focus, clarity, and sustainable delivery practices
- Escalates when team capacity, technical debt, or dependencies threaten milestones

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural direction, technical quality guidance, and implementation oversight to ensure solutions meet both business goals and engineering standards.

### Responsibilities
- Guide system design, architecture, and key technical trade-offs
- Review technical feasibility, complexity, and implementation risks
- Support developers through design feedback and code-level guidance
- Help ensure consistency with standards, architecture, and maintainability goals
- Partner with Product and Project stakeholders on decisions with engineering impact

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound solutions that are maintainable and scalable
- Minimize rework caused by poor design or unclear technical direction
- Improve decision quality for complex engineering choices

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Dependency planning with engineering managers and project leads
- Risk and mitigation updates for critical design decisions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to balance customer value with technical constraints
- Coordinates with Engineering Managers on delivery readiness and staffing needs
- Provides design direction to Developers and reviews implementation quality
- Advises Project Managers on technical risks, milestones, and dependencies

---

## QA Lead / Test Manager

### Role Summary
The QA Lead defines the quality strategy for the project and ensures that features meet agreed acceptance criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards, test plans, and release-readiness criteria
- Coordinate test strategy for unit, integration, and end-to-end validation
- Review acceptance criteria with Product and engineering stakeholders
- Track defects, quality risks, and release blockers
- Partner with teams to improve confidence in change quality

### Goals
- Reduce defects and release risk
- Strengthen confidence in delivery quality and acceptance criteria
- Align quality validation with customer and business needs

### Typical Communication
- Acceptance criteria reviews and quality sign-off discussions
- Defect triage and validation updates with Developers and PMs
- Release readiness check-ins with stakeholders and sponsors

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to confirm that requirements are testable and measurable
- Coordinates with Developers to validate quality and identify defects early
- Supports Project Managers in readiness planning and release gating
- Escalates quality or regression risks that could affect launch confidence

---

## Security / Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
The Security / Compliance Partner helps the team identify and manage security, privacy, and policy requirements throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review features, workflows, and systems for compliance and security impact
- Help define controls, protections, and risk mitigations
- Advise on secure design patterns and required approvals
- Support incident, audit, and regulatory readiness activities
- Partner with delivery teams on policy exceptions and mitigation plans

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Support healthy, policy-aligned delivery practices
- Increase trust in product readiness and operational safeguards

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and compliance checkpoints
- Risk and control updates during planning and release activities
- Escalation of issues with legal, security, or governance stakeholders

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers and Project Managers to ensure requirements and timelines account for required controls
- Reviews technical design decisions with Technical Leads and Developers
- Contributes to release readiness discussions and deployment risk checks
- Helps escalate risks when policy gaps or security issues threaten delivery or compliance

---

## Customer Success / Support Liaison

### Role Summary
The Customer Success / Support Liaison connects product delivery to real customer experience, support needs, and readiness considerations.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback, support trends, and service-impact signals
- Help translate customer experience issues into backlog or readiness priorities
- Coordinate with delivery teams on launch readiness and communication needs
- Highlight adoption, supportability, and customer-impact concerns
- Support stakeholder alignment on customer-facing outcomes

### Goals
- Improve customer outcomes and product adoption
- Reduce support friction caused by unclear rollout or readiness gaps
- Ensure customer realities inform prioritization and release decisions

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and issue escalations
- Release readiness and launch coordination with PMs and stakeholders
- Support and adoption briefings during milestone reviews

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Shares customer insight with Product Managers to refine priorities and success metrics
- Provides feedback to Project Managers on deployment readiness and communication needs
- Helps Technical Leads and Developers understand user-impact considerations for edge cases or support issues
- Supports Stakeholders and sponsors with a customer-impact view during reviews

---

## Business Sponsor / Stakeholder Owner

### Role Summary
The Business Sponsor / Stakeholder Owner provides strategic sponsorship, approves trade-offs, and ensures the initiative remains aligned with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define strategic intent, success expectations, and business priority
- Approve scope trade-offs, budget, and key milestone decisions
- Champion cross-functional alignment and executive communication
- Resolve conflicts that span teams or business units
- Support prioritization decisions tied to outcomes and stakeholder value

### Goals
- Keep the project aligned with business value and strategic goals
- Support timely decisions that reduce delays or ambiguity
- Maintain sponsorship for delivery and adoption outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Executive updates and milestone reviews
- Decisions on scope, risk, and strategic trade-offs
- Alignment meetings with PMs, Product Leads, and senior stakeholders

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to confirm strategic alignment and outcomes
- Collaborates with Project Managers on risk, prioritization, and stakeholder communication
- Provides sponsorship context to Engineering Managers and Technical Leads on key trade-offs
- Helps resolve escalated decisions affecting delivery, scope, or resource commitments

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles help clarify accountability, escalation paths, decision ownership, and cross-functional coordination across the project lifecycle.
